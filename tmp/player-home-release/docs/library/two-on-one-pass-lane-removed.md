# 2-on-1: Pass Lane Removed

## Teaching Point

A 2-on-1 is not automatically a pass.

If the defender takes away the pass lane, the puck carrier should recognize the space that opened elsewhere.

## Main Cue

The defender sits between the puck carrier and support teammate.

## Correct Read

Attack the open shot lane.

## Common Mistake

Forcing the puck through the defender because the player thinks every 2-on-1 needs to become a pass.

## Age-Band Language

### U7/U9

- "The pass is blocked."
- "Use the open space."

### U11/U13

- "The pass lane is closed."
- "Attack the open space."

### U15/U18

- "D1 removes F2."
- "F1 keeps attack through the open shot lane."
