
# Backcheck Recovery

Backcheck recovery is the trailing defender or forward's read when returning into a developing rush.

## Objective Read

The backchecker should identify and remove the most dangerous inside support option instead of chasing the puck from behind.

## Authoring Notes

- Show the inside support player as the danger.
- Show why chasing the puck from behind leaves the middle open.
- Keep the read spatial and objective.
