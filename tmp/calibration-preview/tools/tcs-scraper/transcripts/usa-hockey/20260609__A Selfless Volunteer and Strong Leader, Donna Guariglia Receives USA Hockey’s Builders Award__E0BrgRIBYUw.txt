It's not often that one's vision and dedication helps shape the trajectory of an organization. But for Donna Garriglia, one of the true architects of hockey's growth in the United States, her legacy is just that. Born and raised in a hockey family, Garriglia has spent her entire life around the game, turning her passion for the sport into vision and action. Serving as a volunteer for her hometown New Jersey Colonials program, including as board president, she was the driving force behind the creation of the Colonials girls hockey >> >> which is thriving to this day. And also founded the Mid-Atlantic Women's Hockey Association, an effort to grow grassroots opportunities for female players throughout the Atlantic District. In the more than two decades since, girls and women's hockey has grown nearly 120% in the Atlantic District, a testament to the leadership, commitment, and foresight of the Morristown, New Jersey native. Garriglia's passion for service didn't stop in New Jersey, and in 2008, she joined USA Hockey at the national level in a variety of different positions, including a five-year tenure as the chair of the girls and women's section. Garriglia has also been a leading voice through the years in helping advance USA Hockey's diversity efforts, serving as chair of the organization's committee charged with furthering initiatives aimed at building more welcoming environments for those interested in hockey across the country. In 2015, Garriglia was elected as treasurer of USA Hockey, the first female officer in the history of the organization, a position she retires from tomorrow. During her tenure, she has helped guide USA Hockey during a period defined by growth and international success, but also through unprecedented challenges, notably the COVID-19 pandemic, presiding always with unwavering leadership, transparency, and long-term vision for the organization. The Builders Award recognizes those who don't just contribute to the game, but who help construct its future. Gariglietti has laid a foundation upon which generations of players, coaches, officials, and volunteers will continue to build. A true steward of the game who has made an indelible mark on USA Hockey. Please join us in congratulating the 2026 Builders Award winner, Donna Gariglietti.
