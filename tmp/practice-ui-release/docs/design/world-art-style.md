## What stays consistent

- An elevated three-quarter view of a small, self-contained winter world, with visible terrain depth and coherent scale.
- Carefully illustrated, dimensional environments: rich material detail and soft atmospheric depth, with readable large shapes at thumbnail size.
- Deep midnight-navy surroundings; snowy rock platforms, evergreen trees, frosted architecture and luminous blue ice.
- Warm amber windows and lanterns against cool cyan ice paths. Gold is a restrained warm accent, not an all-over filter.
- A clearly identifiable landmark and an inviting ice route leading into it. Every world feels like a place to explore.
- Soft cast shadows, cool ambient light, warm local light sources and subtle glow. Preserve highlights and dark separation instead of flattening everything into blue.
- Quiet outer edges that blend into the app's dark navy surfaces, with space for readable interface elements outside the artwork.

The six domains may have different landmarks and mood accents. Keep their camera language, material treatment, detail level, snow, lighting and overall palette related.

## What may change

World theme, landmark, route shape, architecture, local props and small environmental storytelling details can vary with the hockey concept. The visual family remains the reference sheet above. Future changes to that family require Thomas's explicit direction.

Do not drift into flat vector icons, low-poly toys, plastic mobile-game scenery, unrelated photoreal landscapes, neon cyberpunk, oversaturated candy colours or a new camera perspective. Do not put text, badges, mission nodes, player avatars or progress numbers into the source environment image; the interface owns those layers.

Always supply the actual reference image to the generation/editing tool when supported. A text prompt alone is insufficient for consistent visual matching. Generated candidates can still vary; the next review determines whether they belong in this family.

## Check before integrating

Compare the actual candidate beside the master at full size and at its real card size on desktop and phone. Check the viewpoint, landmark scale, cool/warm balance, terrain detail, glow, edge blending and legibility of the interface over/around it. Fix visible style drift before integration. Record the candidate path and hash with any approval preview; do not present a recreated mockup as the implemented artwork.

Worlds organize learning. Their decoration must not imply that missions, awards or learning outcomes exist before those functions and their evidence are implemented.
