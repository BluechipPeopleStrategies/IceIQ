# RinkReads content review — handoff instructions

Open `2026-09-18-foundations-review-pack.html` in a browser. The pack includes all exact text and drawings and works offline. The optional local-app link requires Thomas's running development server.

Choose your review role, enter your name/date, then review each item for the ages and game formats you can assess:

- Coach: 52 items — 22 landmarks, six roles/responsibilities and 24 map states.
- Equipment helper: 13 gear categories and their purpose/fit boundaries.
- Qualified official: six signal drawings, movement captions and call explanations.

For every reviewed item, select Accept, Revise or Hold, select the ages checked, and describe the applicable game format or conditions. Revise/Hold requires a note. Accept means accept only for the specified ages/formats, not for every possible league or use.

Use Download review JSON to return a saved copy. Device-local saving is only a convenience; keep the downloaded file. Incomplete reviews can be exported with remaining items listed as pending. Nothing is emailed, published or marked production-approved automatically.

The exported fingerprint binds the response to the content/drawing/interaction files packaged here. When those change, regenerate the pack and re-check affected items; do not copy an old acceptance onto a new 3D view. New full-3D assets will have their own review milestone.

No real human review has been completed or signed by this handoff. Test exports are kept separately under work and are not review evidence.
