```text
Premium realistic contemporary hockey-game character art, convincing three-dimensional shape, natural anatomy under substantial protective gear, detailed fabric/leather/seams/straps, readable black cloth and restrained specular highlights. The goalie faces the camera, in a balanced set stance: knees bent, hips slightly hinged, chest lifted, feet apart, gloves forward. The blade rests in front of the pads at ice level. Never swap sides. Do not put a second stick into the catching glove.

Center the entire goalie with ample transparent room around catcher, blocker, skates and full stick; at least 10 percent clear padding. No clipped equipment or shadows. Keep the complete silhouette and real negative spaces crisp. Deliver only this single transparent character asset.
```
