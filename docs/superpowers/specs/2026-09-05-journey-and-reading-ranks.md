# RinkReads journey and reading ranks

Owner direction, September 5: replace low-quality world tiles and icons, make the digital skill path useful off the ice, add meaningful adventure-style progression, reward consistency and demonstrated understanding, and deliver a few sourced facts daily. This extends the authorized scenario/visual work; the coach dashboard follows scenario coherence.

## Current implementation decision

Use the existing 31-concept, age-banded curriculum as the mission spine. Keep concept IDs, answer keys, existing path records and training records. Present its six domains as original hockey worlds, with professionally illustrated winter environments, connected mission nodes and one obvious next mission. Each node describes a visible decision: `edges-balance` becomes **Spot who can react**, not a claim that an online quiz taught skating balance. Physical practice and coach observations stay separate.

The home screen has one primary journey. The old 64-level activity-count map is retained as an optional activity-history view, rather than competing with the mission path. Its historic counters are not migrated into demonstrated knowledge. Current mission stars still describe quiz performance (50/80/100 percent), not physical skill mastery. Remove unrelated-question fallback for concept missions and do not award a mission result from questions outside its concept. Unavailable missions state that more questions are being prepared.

## Progression

Recognize → choose → revisit → apply is the intended learning loop. A successful practice mission opens the next mission. Replays are always available and never remove stars. The visual final node of a world celebrates completing its existing concept missions. A fresh multi-concept end-of-world challenge needs source-pinned questions and should not be represented as implemented until it runs and stores its own evidence.

A separate reading rank can recognize both consistency and understanding. Proposed thresholds are Starter (entry), Scout (3 distinct practice days and 1 demonstrated concept), Playmaker (7 days and 3 concepts), Game Reader (14 days and 6 concepts), Captain (30 days and 12 concepts). A demonstrated concept means at least five distinct current-catalog questions, with at least 80% correct on the earliest recorded response to each question. Date/answer records without a resolvable current concept do not become knowledge evidence. Count days with completed answers; several sessions on one day count once. Rank does not change Game Sense Score. These are product progression rules, not a scientific competency scale or a physical hockey certification.

The thresholds need observation with real users and adequate question coverage. No paid subscription advantage, purchased advancement, lives, loss of earned progress or public child leaderboard. Old practice points do not silently become a rank. New questions and later recall checks are required for stronger claims about transfer or retained understanding.

## Visual system

Original six-world winter artwork, navy glass surfaces, restrained gold highlights, one SVG icon grid, legible labels and 44px minimum controls. The bottom navigation is a compact centered glass dock on desktop and phone. Motion is brief, optional and respects reduced motion. The rink's Pass/Shoot/Carry action cues have equal visual weight; position tags stay smaller.

## Study scale

Daily Hockey Intel offers a small date-stable set rather than an endless shuffle. Source plan and verified candidate inventory are tracked separately in `2026-09-05-study-library-source-plan.md`. Unique facts, question variants and scenarios are counted independently; no claim of thousands of verified facts until that inventory exists.
