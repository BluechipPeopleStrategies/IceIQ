# U7-U11 source discovery and question seeds

**Checked:** 2026-09-05. **Status:** research candidates for the requested diverse scenario catalog; no question, tactical claim or scenario is approved by this file. **Scope:** U7-U11 foundations, public primary coaching sources, retained federation documents. Jack Han's *Hockey Tactics 2026* is a separate source lane; its paid book was not accessed here.

These are **20 original topic/question seeds**, not 20 complete or validated situations. The draft age ranges are authoring recommendations grounded in the named age frameworks, not claims that the sources tested these digital questions. Interaction suggestions are proposals, not an assertion that every adapter exists.

## What "common questions" means here

Hockey Canada's U7/U9 FAQ pages explicitly publish recurring program questions, including small-ice rules and goalie rotation. Their frequency, search volume and frequency among children are unknown. Skills repeated across federation curricula provide a second kind of signal: recurring teaching topics. Neither signal supplies a measured ranking of questions asked by U7, U9 or U11 players. No forum popularity has been used to establish a correct hockey answer.

## Conflicts and holds for the catalog compiler

- **U9 transition-date conflict:** HC-U9-FAQ says full-ice transition is permitted December 26 or later; HC-U9-RULES describes January 15 or later. Both were live on the check date. Record the conflict and resolve current Hockey Alberta/Member policy before any date-dependent rule or broad U9 eligibility key. Do not silently choose one.
- **Age tags are context:** generic all-age coaching principles do not mean every U7 child should receive a multi-player system. Use one visible cue and declared game orientation for beginners. U11 matrix membership is topic eligibility, not independent proof of an answer.
- **Rules and safety:** these pages are program guidance. Final scored restarts, penalties or contact lessons need the applicable current jurisdiction/program receipt and visible state; the 2019 US manual cannot establish current Canadian penalty rules.
- **Backcheck core:** the detailed vault concept says highest-threat assignment remains synthesis rather than a directly verified claim. F20 stays held. Do not treat a topic list as corroboration of that exact rule.
- **Mechanics are separate evidence:** picture labels, matching and event recognition test what is pictured. They do not certify balance, skating technique, safe equipment fit, live scanning behavior or player-transfer effects.
- **No mass-output claim:** these seeds still need novelty comparison against existing content, claim/age admission, original geometry and timing, question answerability, required tactical/physics gates and the designated review. Different question types alone do not create different scenarios.
