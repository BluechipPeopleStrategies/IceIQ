## Authoring implications

Use explicitly stated role/system conditions, visible cues and coherent puck ownership. Keep observation answers separate from suggested coaching answers. A position comparison may have several useful placements; a static illustration does not establish skating time, interception certainty or retained understanding. Vary the actual decision context, not just the wording, side of the rink or player labels. Rules-dependent special-team cases require a named jurisdiction/competition and fresh rule receipt before a rules answer is authored.
