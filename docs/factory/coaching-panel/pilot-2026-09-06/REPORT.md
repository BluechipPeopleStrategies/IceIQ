## Reviewer performance changed the operating rules

The Luna lead did not qualify to clear hockey judgments. Its eight historical verdicts were five repairs and three retains, contradicting its own four/four summary; some detections were partial. Its current review also contained incorrect actor roles and example coordinates. Its all-retain result was therefore advisory. A stronger Sol reviewer reviewed all 34 questions independently.

Sol detected seven historical defects and partially detected the eighth. It also made a current arithmetic error: it claimed two players were equally close in the U9 scene. Root recalculation found Gold1 at 9.18 m and Navy2 at 11.80 m. That repair was withdrawn. The original review and correction are preserved separately. Effective totals are 29 retain and five repair, not the original 28/six.

Use economical models for bounded preparation and source retrieval with checked output. Substantive review requires successful calibration, independent verification of risky claims and escalation after failures. Even strong models need arithmetic checks and human calibration; model confidence is not evidence.
