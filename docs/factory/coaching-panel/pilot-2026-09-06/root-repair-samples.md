# Root repair samples for adjudication

Drafts only. These are proposed wording changes, not published replacements or final coach approval. Reconcile with the independent review and preserve before/after hashes when serializing.

## Retrieval q8: make the distractor fit the hypothetical

Keep the stem that explicitly assumes recovery and a planned pass to F2. Replace 'The puck remains loose beside the wall' with **'D1 moves away from the passing lane.'** The learner compares a change that weakens the outlet with one that may improve it. A still-loose puck contradicts the premise and makes the old choice trivial.

## U11 turning q5: ask what the changed cue creates

Proposed stem: **'Suppose Gold1 moves toward Gold2, leaving the lane YOU face. Which new option does that space give YOU to assess?'**

- Move closer to Navy2.
- Carry into the space Gold1 left.
- Keep holding the puck where YOU are.

Suggested answer: the carrying option. Feedback: **'Gold1 leaving that lane creates space YOU can assess for a carry. Holding or turning toward support may still be reasonable, but neither is the new option created by that change. Recheck the other players before committing.'** No promised skating success or universal command.

## U13 cue question: assess actual relationships

Proposed stem: **'Which two relationships are visible here?'**

- Gold1 is closer to YOU than Navy2 is.
- Navy2 is farther toward the side boards than YOU are.
- Gold2 is between YOU and Navy2.

The first two are supported by the frozen coordinates; Gold2 is across the ice. This becomes a scene-based observation, not a claim that a still frame can assess a pace change. It should accompany a narrower pressure/support learning objective, or the entire pace-change task needs a movement stimulus. Do not silently retitle only one question and leave contradictory metadata.

## Batch-level answer cue

Balance the rendered location of keyed choices only after the substantive options are sound. Keep stable option IDs and preserve response/hash versioning. Answer shuffling does not turn an obviously unreasonable distractor into a useful one.
