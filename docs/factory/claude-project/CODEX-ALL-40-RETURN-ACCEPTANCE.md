## Findings to carry into future authoring

- The original U18 penalty kill called the wrong attacker the backdoor threat. Read actual team, puck and role assignments before writing communication answers.
- A turnover question referred to the first turnover when the freeze was after the second. Hypothetical events do not automatically change another question's initial frame.
- A point-cover example moved toward the net. A net-side example was farther from the net than its threat. A loose-puck approach moved away from the actual puck. Measure the final coordinates, not the intended teaching label.
- Independent reviewers also missed defects and miscalculated distances. Recheck their criticism against serialized data. A second AI pass is not a human coaching endorsement.
- Use explicit manpower counts for full special-team examples. For a selected-player view, state which players are omitted when the visible count could contradict the named game state.
- Scanning, support and coverage remain conditional. A point on the rink cannot guarantee gaze, a clear pass, possession or completed coverage.

All content remains experimental. Human coach approval, exhaustive visual review and curriculum/mastery admission are separate work. Supabase was not part of this release.
