# Source scope for the companion lessons

Retrieved September 7, 2026. These public sources inform original teaching themes; none certifies the authored answer keys, geometry, or review quality. No proprietary templates or purchased-book text were reproduced.

- [Hockey Canada U9 fundamental skills](https://www.hockeycanada.ca/en-ca/hockey-programs/coaching/under-9/coaches/skills), sections Fundamental Skill Development and Moving Passing and Receiving: age-appropriate fundamentals, moving passes, and pass-and-follow. Used to keep the U9 lessons about receiving and support in a small-area exercise. These are Canadian developmental recommendations, not universal North American competition rules.
- [Hockey Canada Vision and Scanning](https://www.hockeycanada.ca/en-ca/news/seven-coaching-principles-vision-scanning-2024-ncw), published September 18, 2024, sections Key Information to Identify and Teaching Puck Control: observe teammates and pressure, interpret information, and adapt decisions. Used as the general theme for the older lessons; not evidence that an unanimated turn succeeded or created space.
- [Hockey Canada U7 FAQ](https://www.hockeycanada.ca/en-ca/hockey-programs/coaching/under-7/faq), sections full-ice games and cross-ice dimensions: U7 uses a size-appropriate cross-ice playing surface. The full rink here is a landmark orientation diagram, not a U7 full-ice game or an offside lesson.

Exact actor positions, possession and rink landmarks come from the repository's scene builder and renderer. Those checks are separate from documentary support. Different local rules and team systems require explicit context before a rule-dependent question is admitted.
