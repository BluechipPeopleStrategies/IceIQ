# Experimental review and local development integration

The owner resumed this work after the earlier pause. The 100 original scenarios and 600 questions now exist in the separate experimental bank. The durable review result and open content queue are in [the critical coaching audit](../factory/research/question-review/README.md).

## Local implementation

- Shared camera presets, direct rotation/pan/zoom controls, focus/full-rink framing and optional rink-area names. Accessible buttons remain available. Player-facing examples, marker placement, puck locator scale and ten-skater layout editing were updated.
- Six preserved world illustrations and a new player-home module are connected to the learning/practice routes. Old journey history remains accessible as activity history.
- Spaced-practice bookkeeping uses five distinct eligible questions, five practice dates, two calendar weeks, at least seven elapsed days and 80% accuracy as configurable product defaults. Existing history is retained; experimental answers do not award mastery. The clock measures practice distribution, not reaction speed.
- GoalBuilder replaces the old SMART text wizard in the allowed goal route, with starters, an observable action/measure, opportunities, a reason, review dates, drafts and check-ins. Optional perceived exertion is a self-report, not a quality score. Rich plans/check-ins remain device-local while existing SMART text retains its sync path.
- CoachAssessment preserves age-specific rating anchors, requires dated evidence for changed ratings, and separates shared examples from private notes. Higher ratings require evidence across dates and a game-related context. Legacy ratings are retained without fabricated evidence.
- Experimental question workshop offers all-question browsing, a focused flag queue, versioned response context, before/after editing, choice-format revision, validation and draft export. Saving a revision draft does not publish it. Original approved-bank review tools remain available at their old routes.

## Verification and limits

The practice suite passed 518 tests, including the final draft-format validation test. The 29 goal/home/coach tests passed, and the production build completed with chunk-size warnings. The coverage audit validates all 600 first reviews, all required independent second reviews and the four revised question hashes. The final reconciled queue count is generated by that audit.

Actual browser checks covered experimental age filtering and deep links; a position response restored on the same question after reload; question flag and revision-draft persistence; the unchanged practice question after saving a draft; desktop broadcast/overhead framing; phone workshop/home/goal/coach layouts without horizontal overflow; a goal check-in save; and the coach's evidence-required save followed by successful save/reopen with the dated example retained. These checks used isolated demo/preview profiles. They do not certify every one of the 100 rendered scenes or a physical tablet.

Browser testing found and repaired the coach lazy-load boundary and the first-assessment case with no cached notes. A regression test covers the missing cache and preservation of another coach's changed notes. Revised scenarios can use version 2; validation rejects invalid/non-integer versions and old responses do not silently become answers to new versions.

The narrator remains browser text-to-speech with existing coach-persona choices. A consistent recorded young-adult narrator has not been produced or auditioned; no such delivery is claimed.

## Repository boundary

The task began with substantial unrelated or paused work already in the working tree. The content/review commit captures the new bank, audit receipts, validator and task-list update. The broader presentation/home/goals/coach integrations remain local working-tree changes for a separately scoped release; they must not be swept into a content-audit commit. Temporary task-start backups remain under the named rink-camera-facing and rink-spaced-mastery folders in the user's Temp directory. No local memory files were edited.
