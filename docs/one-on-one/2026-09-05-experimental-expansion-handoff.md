## Local practice behavior

The working application presents 1,500 routine questions including 200 optional reflections (13.3%). Another 100 reflections remain in the authoring bank and can be opened directly. Question navigation shows numbers; answer targets retain a 44px minimum. Shaded rink regions and a puck legend replace label pills and repeated puck callouts. The opening camera frames the relevant play; movement does not reset the camera.

Local analytics record scenario ID, version, question ID and content hash with views, checks, retries, reflection skips, flags and camera choices. The report exposes sample sizes, storage status and export. It does not collect reflection text or provide a cross-player data service. Older-version responses do not certify repaired questions.

The six learning worlds have accessible winding mission paths with explicit visited state keyed to profile, age and world. Visits do not fabricate mastery. Original world artwork is preserved.
