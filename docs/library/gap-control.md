
# Gap Control

Gap control is the defender's management of space between the attacker and the dangerous middle lane.

## Objective Read

When the attacker enters with speed, the defender should hold inside position, manage space, and protect the middle instead of reaching early or backing straight in.

## Authoring Notes

- Show the attacker threatening the middle.
- Show the defender with enough inside position to make the correct read objective.
- Do not rely on color alone.

  concepts. https://cdn1.sportngin.com/attachments/document/0066/4690/Skill_Progression_Manual_19_FINAL.pdf

  control in the Individual Defensive Tactics matrix at the same ages.
- Sacilotto, "7 Rules of Defensive Hockey" (The Coaches Site) — the
  readiness/moving-stance framing (a flat-footed defender can't adjust the
  gap) behind `GAP_CONTROL_PIVOT_MATCH_PLAY`.

Both existing plays (`GAP_CONTROL_HOLD_PLAY`, `GAP_CONTROL_PIVOT_MATCH_PLAY`)
were authored before this research and are confirmed evidence-consistent, not
contradicted by it.
