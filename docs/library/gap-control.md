
# Gap Control

Gap control is the defender's management of space between the attacker and the dangerous middle lane.

## Objective Read

When the attacker enters with speed, the defender should hold inside position, manage space, and protect the middle instead of reaching early or backing straight in.

## Authoring Notes

- Show the attacker threatening the middle.
- Show the defender with enough inside position to make the correct read objective.
- Do not rely on color alone.
