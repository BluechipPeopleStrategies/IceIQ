
# Forecheck Pressure

Forecheck pressure is the first pressure player's read when trying to remove time, space, and dangerous outlets.

## Objective Read

The first forechecker should angle pressure to take away the middle and force the puck carrier toward a predictable lower-danger option.

## Second Read: The Reverse

After the carrier is forced one way, the first forechecker should finish on an angle that takes away the reverse (the turn-back pass to a support player behind the play) so the breakout stays predictable instead of flipping away from the pressure.
